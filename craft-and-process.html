02 · EXTRACTION PROCESS

How a floral
absolute is made

01

Fresh in.

Flowers arrive in temperature-controlled vehicles and are inspected and cleaned.

02

Gentle extraction.

A food-grade solvent draws out the aromatic compounds, leaving a rich floral concrete.

03

Purified cold.

The concrete is purified with alcohol at around -40°C, where waxes separate out.

04

Pure absolute.

The alcohol is gently recovered, leaving a pure floral absolute.

05

Verified.

Every batch is tested with GC-MS analysis and approved by our quality team before it leaves.

05 · CAPTIVE AROMA CHEMICALS

Captive Aroma Chemicals
Original chemistry. Distinctive signatures.

Developed in our laboratories and reserved for our formulations, our captive aroma molecules give our perfumers a palette beyond commercially available ingredients. Around ten are already in active use, bringing proprietary innovation into the fragrances we create.

Their chemistry remains confidential, preserving an exclusive creative resource for our perfumers. For our clients, this means distinctive olfactive signatures with an additional layer of differentiation—making them more difficult to replicate.

Our research also extends to enzymatic synthesis, transforming selected natural materials into new aroma ingredients. By exploring new possibilities within familiar ingredients, we broaden our creative palette and bring original dimensions to fragrance design.
