French Craft. Indian Rigour.

Our French connection is a partnership, not a postcode.

Our experience working in France has shaped our approach to perfumery, giving us a first-hand understanding of its creative discipline, technical precision and attention to detail. Through our partnership with French perfumers, we draw on this expertise to create fragrances that unite our Indian heritage with a French creative perspective.

Conceived and designed in France, these fragrances come to life at our manufacturing facility in India. Here, our knowledge of ingredients and production expertise preserve the integrity of each creation while delivering consistency, scale and efficiency.

For our clients, this brings two complementary strengths together: the artistry of French fragrance design and the capability of Indian manufacturing, without a compromise between creative ambition and commercial value.
