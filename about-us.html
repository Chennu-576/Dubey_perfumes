HISTORICAL CHRONOLOGY · 1895 — PRESENT
01 / 08 — 1895
Founded in Kannauj

Dubey Perfumers · Heritage

01
1895
Founded in Kannauj

Devi Prasad Dubey establishes the business in Kannauj, India’s fragrance capital, laying the foundations of a family-led fragrance house. Early clients include officials of the British Empire.

02
1960s
Expansion to Calcutta

We relocate to Calcutta, expanding our capabilities in natural distillation, fragrance compounding and international trade. Our sandalwood oil business grows to become one of India’s largest exporters to the United States.

03
1970s
A New Base in Hyderabad

We move to Hyderabad, a major centre for sandalwood procurement. Closer to the source, we strengthen our operations and establish ourselves as one of India’s largest sandalwood businesses.

04
Late 1980s
Fragrance at the Core

Building on decades of expertise in natural ingredients, we make fragrance creation the central focus of our business. This strategic evolution sets the foundation for a new chapter of growth.

05
1990s–2005
Leadership in Indian Fragrance

We emerge as one of India’s leading fragrance houses, combining creative expertise with manufacturing scale. Strong customer partnerships and a deep understanding of the Indian market establish our position as a trusted industry partner.

06
After 2005
Our French Connection

We expand into France, establishing Only Fragrances in partnership with a senior French perfumer. Dedicated to fine fragrance, the company brings French creative expertise into our business, connecting our Indian heritage with an international perfumery perspective.

07
2016–2022
Built for International Growth

We develop our Hyderabad fine-fragrance facility, bringing French-inspired working practices together with Indian manufacturing strength. The facility marks a new chapter in our ability to create, develop and produce fragrances for international markets.

08
Today
A Global Fragrance House

We create fine fragrances for brands across the globe, uniting Indian heritage, French expertise and modern manufacturing. Still led by our founding family, we continue to grow through innovation, quality and enduring partnerships.
