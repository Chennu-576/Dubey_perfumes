Story 01 · Mecca · Medina

A fragrance for the holy mosque

We were entrusted with an innovative brief: to develop a specialised antibacterial fragrance exclusively for use at the holy mosques in Mecca and Medina. It needed to fulfil its antibacterial purpose without bringing a clinical atmosphere into a sacred place.

People were arriving for worship. The fragrance needed to respect that moment. This meant approaching the formulation with equal attention to its technical requirements and the experience of those encountering it.

We spent 18 months developing and refining the fragrance, with testing conducted through specialist laboratories in India involving approximately 200 people. Following approval from the relevant Saudi medical authority, it was introduced into diffusers at the entrances—a bespoke creation with a specific antibacterial purpose, designed to feel entirely at home in its setting.

Story 02 · Royal Family of the Gulf

Made for Royalty

Among our most exclusive creations is a bespoke blend developed for one of the Gulf’s ruling royal families. Drawing on our expertise in natural ingredients, we shaped the composition through extensive research and successive rounds of feedback, refining it to the royal house’s precise preferences.

The distinction lies in how it was created: not an existing fragrance presented to royalty, but an original composition developed exclusively for them. Its exclusivity began with the brief—not the label.

Story 03 · Captive Molecule

The Molecule That Made the Difference

A leading national fragrance brand, and a client of four years, approached us with a brief for a major launch: a fruity fragrance with room-filling presence and lasting power.

Five months into development, we were close. The fragrance had the direction we were looking for, but something was still missing—a final element to bring the composition together.

We turned to our proprietary palette, introducing one of our captive molecules to give the composition a more distinctive signature. The revised fragrance was approved and successfully launched—bringing our research out of the laboratory and into a meaningful point of difference for the brand.
